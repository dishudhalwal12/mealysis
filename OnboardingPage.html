ABOUT MEALYSIS

Mealysis is a web-based solution designed to simplify product and restaurant price analysis. It helps users compare prices across aggregator platforms and calculate real profits with AI insights. Developed by Onkar Singh, Sameer Negi, and Divanshu Maheshwari as part of their BCA Major Project at JIMS, Vasant Kunj.
